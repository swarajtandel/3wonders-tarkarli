Welcome to Wairkar's 3 Wonders

A Little Piece of Tarkarli

Discover a home away from home at Wairkar's 3 Wonders, where every stay is an experience and every meal is a celebration. Nestled along the pristine Tarkarli Devbag Road, just steps from Tarkarli Beach, our property offers the perfect blend of coastal comfort and authentic Malvani hospitality.

Enjoy our thoughtfully designed rooms paired with a curated menu of local cuisine. Whether you're here for a peaceful family vacation, a romantic getaway, or an adventure-filled weekend - Wairkar's 3 Wonders promises a stay that lingers in memory.

Meet Your Host

About the Owner

Welcome to Wairkar's 3 Wonders Tarkarli. Our family has deep roots in Malvan, and we are passionate about sharing the beauty of Tarkarli and our authentic coastal culture with guests from all over the world.

We personally ensure that every guest feels at home, offering tailored recommendations for exploring the hidden gems of Sindhudurg, from secret beaches to the finest Malvani culinary experiences.
