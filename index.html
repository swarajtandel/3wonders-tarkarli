A Boutique Coastal Stay

Wairkar's 3 Wonders Tarkarli: Your Beachside Sanctuary

Located along the picturesque Tarkarli-Devbag coastal corridor in Malvan, Wairkar's 3 Wonders is built for slow weekends, sun-soaked beaches, and warm Konkan hospitality. Designed for families, couples, and groups seeking tranquil coastal vibes, our boutique property offers spacious, fully air-conditioned rooms with private bathrooms, secure parking, and round-the-clock front desk assistance.

Wake up to the gentle sea breeze, stroll down to Tarkarli Beach in less than two minutes, and spend your afternoons relaxing in our peaceful courtyard or discovering the vibrant coastal culture of Sindhudurg.

In-House Culinary Journey

Savour & Unwind: Authentic Malvani Flavours

A breezy seaside table, the aroma of stone-ground Konkan spices, and a menu celebrating the bounty of the Arabian Sea. Meals at 3 Wonders are an invitation to slow down and relish authentic Malvani gastronomy.

From freshly caught Surmai and Pomfret pan-fried to crisp perfection, to comforting Kokum-infused curries and chilled glasses of freshly pressed Sol Kadhi, every dish is crafted with local home-style love. Gather your loved ones and make every meal a coastal memory.

Discover Konkan Magic

Let the Konkan Coast Captivate Your Heart

There is something quietly enchanting about Tarkarli. From the magnificent sea walls of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj's 17th-century Sindhudurg Fort rising majestically from the Arabian Sea, to dolphin cruises across the peaceful Karli backwaters, this coastal haven is steeped in natural wonder and Maratha history.

Whether you are scuba diving among vibrant underwater corals or watching golden sunsets over Devbag Beach, 3 Wonders provides the ideal base to experience it all.

Devbag Beach

Heritage

Sindhudurg Fort

Historic sea bastion built by Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj in 1664. Ferry ride from Malvan jetty.

Tsunami Island

Naturally formed sand island in the Karli delta offering jet-skiing, bumper rides, and banana boats.
