Travel Options

How to Reach 3 Wonders

Tarkarli is easily accessible by flight, railway, and scenic coastal highways.

By Air

Sindhudurg Chipi Airport (SDW): ~22 km (~35 min drive via Malvan).

Mopa Airport, North Goa (GOX): ~90 km (~2 hours).

Dabolim Airport, Goa (GOI): ~130 km (~3 hours drive via NH 66).

By Railway

Kudal Railway Station (KRCL): ~30 km (~45 min taxi/auto rickshaw ride).

Sindhudurg Station: ~32 km.

Regular express trains connect via Konkan Railway from Mumbai (CST/LTT), Pune, Goa, and Mangalore.

By Road

Connected via the scenic Mumbai-Goa Highway (NH 66).

From Mumbai: ~500 km (~9-10 hrs).
From Pune: ~390 km (~7-8 hrs via Kolhapur-Gaganbawda Ghat).
From Goa: ~100 km (~2.5 hrs).
