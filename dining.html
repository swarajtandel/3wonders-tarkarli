Authentic Malvani Dining

From fresh catch pan-fried seafood to soothing glasses of Sol Kadhi, discover the rich flavours and warm hospitality of the Konkan coast.

Coastal Gastronomy

Homestyle Recipes, Fresh Coastal Ingredients

At Wairkar's 3 Wonders Tarkarli, our in-house restaurant is dedicated to serving authentic Konkan and Malvani preparations made with traditional stone-ground masalas, freshly grated coconut, and fish procured directly from the morning catch.

Whether enjoying a leisurely lunch after a morning swim at Tarkarli Beach or relaxing with family over dinner, our dining room and room service provide satisfying, freshly prepared meals made to order.

Coastal Seafood Highlights

Surmai Rava / Masala Fry
Kingfish marinated in authentic Malvani spices and crisp rava fried.
Pomfret Coastal Curry
Fresh white pomfret simmered in coconut milk, triphala, and kokum gravy.
Prawns Sukka / Masala
Tender fresh prawns sautéed with roasted coconut, onions, and Malvani masala.
Malvani Fish Thali
Complete coastal feast with fish curry, fried fish, sol kadhi, bhakri/chapati, and rice.

Traditional Konkan & Veg

Sol Kadhi (Fresh Kokum Drink)
Signature Konkan digestive crafted from pure coconut milk, garlic, and fresh kokum.
Kombdi Vade (Chicken Speciality)
Tender chicken in rich aromatic gravy served with traditional multigrain vade.
Konkan Vegetarian Thali
Homestyle seasonal vegetable curries, dal, kokum kadhi, fresh rotis, and steamed rice.
Coastal Breakfast & Teas
Poha, Upma, Omelettes, and hot tea/coffee to start your morning by the beach.
